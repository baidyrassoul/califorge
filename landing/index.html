Why CaliForge?

The problem

Calisthenics has the best progression system in all of fitness — but it lives in messy spreadsheets, forgotten notes and "which workout app works with bodyweight anyway?" Most apps are generic gym trackers. None feel like the game your progress actually is.

The solution

Built by and for bodyweight athletes, CaliForge treats skills as a real game: a tree to explore, XP to earn, ranks to hold and friends to challenge. No 200-pound machines, no clutter — just your body, your bar and your progress.
