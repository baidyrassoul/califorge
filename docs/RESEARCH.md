# Research & Validation

> Findings from surveys, interviews and market research. Every claim backed by
> data and dated. Update as Phase 0 validation completes.

## Market context (desk research — 2026-08-13)

- Calisthenics equipment market: $0.8 B (2025) → $1.54 B (2034), CAGR 7.2%.
- Outdoor fitness market: $8.6 B (2024) → $17.3 B (2033), CAGR 8.1%.
- Calisthenics audience: ~6.5 M in the US; Japan = 32.9% of worldwide demand.
- Functional-fitness content = ~150 B social views; fitness influencers ~500 M
  combined followers.
- Existing apps: Calisteniapp (2M+ athletes), Calisthenics Family (400k+
  downloads), Thenx, CaliSpot, CaliQuest, Gravity, tracali, Hybrid Calisthenics.

## Existing-app gaps observed (desk research — 2026-08-13)

- **CaliQuest** is the closest concept (skill tree, XP, quests) but is mobile
  native, small (208 reviews), and lacks web presence — opportunity to do it
  better cross-platform with a clean web experience.
- Most apps are **video libraries or generic trackers**, not progression games.
- Several complaints across apps: clunky rep/set logging, no Google Fit/health
  integration, confusing onboarding for beginners.
- Monetization: subscriptions €5–10/month is the accepted norm (Calisteniapp
  9.99€/m, Gravity £7.99/m, CaliSpot Pro £7.99/m).
